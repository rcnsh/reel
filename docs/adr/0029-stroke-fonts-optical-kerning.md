# Stroke fonts are kerned optically and get built typographic glyphs

The single-stroke fonts in `src/engine/stroke.ts` (EMS and Hershey SVG fonts) carry no kerning and no curly quotes, and their `x-height` and `cap-height` attributes are placeholders. So the engine measures and builds what they lack.

- **Metrics**: `parseSvgFont` measures the baseline, x-height and cap height from the outlines of `H` and `x`. Paths use only M and L; y is up in font units, and `parsePath` treats lower-case (relative) commands as absolute since the fonts never use them.
- **Typographic glyphs**: `addTypographic` makes ’ from the font's ' as is (in Hershey the ' is already comma-shaped), ‘ by turning it, “ ” by doubling those, and … from three dots.
- **Kerning** (`pairKern`, `approach`, `profile`): the sidebearings already space plain pairs (nn, oo); only pairs that leave a hole, overhangs and diagonals (To, Yo, We, AV, LT, r.), are kerned. `OPEN_R` lists glyphs whose right side overhangs or slants (they can kern with what follows), `OPEN_L` those whose left side slants or tucks under (they can kern with what precedes). Each glyph's ink is reduced to left and right profiles over `ROWS` (90) horizontal bands, widened so a band sees its neighbours' ink set back by `SHADE` (0.4) times their vertical distance, so a T's arm shades the bands under it. The right glyph moves in until the closest approach over the zone both letters share (x-height for lowercase, cap height otherwise) comes `STRENGTH` (0.6) of the way to the font's own n/o (or H/O) pairs: equal closest approach would pack diagonals and rounds tighter than the eye wants. A second check over the full height (descenders, accents and arms included), with profiles widened at 45°, keeps the inks at least `CLEAR` (0.1 em) apart. Kerns are capped at -0.15 em.
- **Scripts**: the connected scripts (`script`, `hscript`, `SCRIPTS`) are not kerned by default, since kerning would break the joins between letters.
